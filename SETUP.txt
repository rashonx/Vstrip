WHEEL BRIDGE v1.3.0 - Dodge City Plugins
Moza wheel -> Xbox controller, with a constant centring force
=============================================================

ONE-TIME SETUP
1. Open "WheelBridge vX.Y.Z.exe". Windows asks "Do you want to allow this app to make changes?"
   - click Yes. (Wheel Bridge needs this to drive the wheel motor and hide the wheel from games.)
   - Windows may also say "Windows protected your PC" because the app isn't signed.
     Click "More info" then "Run anyway".
2. Click INSTALL DRIVERS in section 1. Wheel Bridge installs its two helper drivers by itself
   (they're built into the app - nothing to download):
     ViGEmBus - lets Windows show the virtual Xbox controller
     HidHide  - lets Wheel Bridge hide the real wheel from games
   Windows only allows drivers to do these two jobs, which is why they're needed.
3. When it's done, unplug the wheel's USB cable and plug it back in, then click Refresh.
   If Wheel Bridge says so, restart your PC once.
4. In Moza Pit House, note your wheel rotation (for example 900 or 540).

HIDING THE WHEEL (automatic)
- With "Hide the wheel from games while running" ticked (on by default), START BRIDGE hides the
  real wheel from games, so they only see the Xbox controller. STOP, or closing Wheel Bridge,
  gives the wheel back to games. If Wheel Bridge is ever closed unexpectedly, it gives the wheel
  back the next time you open it.
- "Hide wheel now" / "Show wheel to games" do it by hand.

EVERY TIME YOU PLAY
1. Open "WheelBridge vX.Y.Z.exe" (the number is the version; delete older copies).
   - Click Yes when Windows asks for permission.
   - Keep your feet off the pedals while it starts (it calibrates their resting position).
2. Your Moza should be picked automatically. The centring force starts right away.
   Use the Centring strength slider. Start low on a direct drive base.
3. Nothing to do for hiding: START BRIDGE hides the real wheel from games automatically.
4. First time only: map your pedals and buttons in section 3.
   - Pedals: click Detect next to Throttle, press the pedal fully. Same for Brake.
   - Buttons: click Set next to an Xbox button, then press the wheel button you want.
   Suggested for Need for Speed Most Wanted:
     A = confirm / handbrake, B = back, X = nitrous, Y = change camera,
     LB / RB = shift paddles, Start = pause menu.
5. Click START BRIDGE, then start Need for Speed Most Wanted.
   The game sees an Xbox 360 controller. Use controller controls in the game menus.

COMBINED PEDAL AXIS (section 3, new in v1.3)
- "To game" picks how gas and brake reach the game:
    Separate (RT gas, LT brake)  - normal Xbox triggers (default)
    Combined on triggers         - gas minus brake: pressing both cancels out
    Combined on right stick      - one axis: stick up = gas, stick down = brake
    Combined on left stick       - same, on the left stick (steering stays left/right)
  Use a combined stick for older games that want gas and brake on one axis. In the game's
  controls, set accelerate / brake to that stick's up / down.
- "Gas + brake on one axis": tick this if your pedals report both on ONE axis that rests in
  the middle. Pick that axis as Throttle (the Brake box is then ignored). Feet off, then press
  gas and brake all the way once each. If they come out backwards, tick Swap.

TUNING
- "Wheel rotation set in Moza Pit House": must match Pit House.
- "Full lock in game at": how far you turn the wheel for full steering in the game.
  NFS is arcade-style, so 270-400 degrees usually feels right.
- "Anti-deadzone": if small wheel movements do nothing in the game, raise this.
- "Damper": adds weight and smoothness.
- "Game rumble (buzz) to wheel": the game's controller rumble as a steady shake.

FORCE FEEDBACK EFFECTS (panel 4, new in v1.1)
Games like Need for Speed only send Xbox rumble, not real steering forces,
so Wheel Bridge works these out from rumble, your pedals and your steering.
Each one has its own on/off box and strength slider.
- Crash & bump kicks: a hit jolts the wheel, then a smaller rebound.
- Road texture: gravel, kerbs and grass come through as a fine fast buzz.
- Speed weight: light when parked, heavier the longer you hold the throttle,
  lighter again when you brake. (It's an estimate; the game doesn't report speed.)
- Kickback: after a big turn, letting the wheel come back fast snaps it to centre.
- Test kick: should push the wheel RIGHT. If it pushes LEFT, tick
  "Flip kick direction" (Moza bases don't all agree on which way is which).

USING THE WHEEL IN OTHER GAMES
Click "Show wheel to games" (or uninstall HidHide) so other games can see the Moza directly.

SAFETY
Direct drive wheels are strong. Keep hands clear when starting the bridge and raise the
centring strength gradually.
