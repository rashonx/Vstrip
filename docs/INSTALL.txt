Rashon Vocal Sitter - install on Windows
========================================

1. Delete any older "Rashon Vocal Sitter ....vst3" folders from the folder below, then copy in the new
   "Rashon Vocal Sitter vX.Y.Z.vst3" folder (the number is the version) to:
       C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\
2. Open your DAW and rescan plugins.

How to set it up (FL Studio)
----------------------------
1. Put Rashon Vocal Sitter on your BEAT mixer track (or the bus your music goes to).
2. Select your VOCAL mixer track. Right-click the little arrow at the bottom of the
   beat track and choose "Sidechain to this track". The vocal keeps playing normally
   through its own track.
3. Open the plugin wrapper (gear icon) > Processing tab and set the sidechain input
   to the vocal's track number.
4. Play the song. You'll see the vocal as gold marks on the display. Turn up AMOUNT
   until the vocal sits on top of the beat.

Other DAWs: put the plugin on the beat track and pick the vocal as its sidechain input.

Controls
--------
AMOUNT     How much space to carve out of the beat (up to 24 dB per band)
ATTACK     How fast the beat ducks when the vocal comes in
RELEASE    How fast the beat comes back when the vocal stops
LOW CUT    Don't carve below this frequency (keep your bass and kick)
HIGH CUT   Don't carve above this frequency
STEREO/MID/SIDE   Carve the whole beat, only its centre, or only its sides
AUTO GAIN  Keeps the beat as loud as it was before carving
OUTPUT     Final level
